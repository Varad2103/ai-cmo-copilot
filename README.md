# ai-cmo-copilot
AI-powered marketing intelligence and executive decision-support platform.

## Overview

AI CMO Copilot is a decision-support system designed to help marketing teams understand:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should we do next?
- What potential business impact could those actions have?

The platform combines marketing analytics, machine learning, customer intelligence, and large language models to generate evidence-based executive insights and recommendations.
